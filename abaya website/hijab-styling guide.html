A LITTLE INSPIRATION

Hijab care & styling

Simple ideas to help you care for your fabrics and find a style that feels comfortable.

01 / FABRIC CARE

Keep delicate scarves looking lovely

Check the care label first. Hand-wash delicate fabrics in cool water with a mild detergent, avoid wringing, and let them air-dry away from direct sunlight.

02 / EASY STYLING

How to use magnetic pins

Place one magnet on each side of the fabric and bring them together gently. Keep magnets away from sensitive devices and small children.

03 / FIT NOTES

Choosing an abaya length

Measure from the top of your shoulder to your preferred hem while wearing the shoes you plan to pair with it. Compare that measurement with the garment details.

04 / GETTING READY

Build an easy everyday set

Start with a comfortable undercap, choose a scarf fabric that suits your day, and keep a couple of reliable pins close by for quick adjustments.
